# Customer Journey & Funnel Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

The Customer Journey & Funnel Analysis Dashboard is a Business Intelligence and Analytics project designed to help organizations understand customer behavior across different stages of the customer lifecycle.

The project focuses on tracking customer interactions, identifying conversion bottlenecks, measuring acquisition channel effectiveness, evaluating customer retention, and uncovering opportunities for revenue growth.

This solution can be used by:

* Business Analysts
* Product Analysts
* Marketing Analysts
* CRM Teams
* Growth Teams
* Management Executives

---

## Business Problem

Organizations spend significant budgets on marketing campaigns and customer acquisition activities.

However, many companies struggle to answer critical questions such as:

* Which acquisition channels bring the most valuable customers?
* Where are customers dropping off in the funnel?
* Which campaigns generate the highest conversions?
* Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?
* How can customer retention be improved?

This dashboard provides data-driven answers to these questions.

---

## Project Objectives

## Key Business Insights

* Checkout stage experienced the highest customer drop-off.
* Paid Advertising generated the highest traffic volume.
* Organic Search delivered the highest conversion rate.
* VIP customers contributed the largest share of revenue.
* Returning customers showed the highest retention rates.
* Certain campaigns significantly outperformed others in conversions.

## Business Recommendations

### Funnel Optimization

### Marketing Strategy
